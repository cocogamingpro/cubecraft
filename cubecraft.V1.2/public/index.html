    Furnace

    Top: what to smelt (raw iron, cobblestone). Bottom: fuel (coal smelts 8 items, wood 4, planks 1). Each item takes 10 seconds, one by one. Diamonds need no smelting. Click an item in your inventory, then click a furnace slot; shift-click sends it to the right slot. Click a furnace slot to take its items out.

    Hotbar

    Click an item to pick it up, then click another slot to move it (stacks merge, different items swap). Shift-click sends a stack to the other half (hotbar / inventory). Hover an item and press 1–9 to swap it with that hotbar slot. To craft, pick up an item and click a crafting cell (right-click a cell to empty it), or click a recipe to fill the grid for you. Click the result to craft once, shift-click to craft as many as you can. Right-click a crafting table to get a 3×3 grid.
