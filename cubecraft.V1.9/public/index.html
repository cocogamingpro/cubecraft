      Furnace

      Top: what to smelt or cook (raw iron, cobblestone, raw pork chop, raw beef, raw mutton). Bottom: fuel (coal smelts 8 items, wood 4, planks 1). Each item takes 10 seconds, one by one. Diamonds need no smelting. Click an item in your inventory, then click a furnace slot; shift-click sends it to the right slot. Click a furnace slot to take its items out.

      Hotbar

      Click an item to pick it up, then click another slot to move it (stacks merge, different items swap). Shift-click sends a stack to the other half (hotbar / inventory). Hover an item and press 1–9 to swap it with that hotbar slot. To craft, pick up an item and click a crafting cell (right-click a cell to empty it), or click a recipe to fill the grid for you. Click the result to craft once, shift-click to craft as many as you can. Right-click a crafting table to get a 3×3 grid. Right-click a chest to store items: click a stack, then click a slot in the chest (or shift-click to send it across). Press Q over a slot to drop one item (Shift+Q: the whole stack).

      Click a block to pick it up, then click a hotbar slot. Or hover a block and press 1–9. Shift-click puts it in your selected slot.
